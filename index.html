Our coverage standards

Practical publishing rules for a new independent high school sports newsroom.

01

Verify every result

Cross-check scores, dates, locations, team names, and classifications with official or primary sources.

02

Make sourcing visible

Add source links and a publication or update time. Correct errors clearly when information changes.

03

Respect people and rights

Use original writing, authorized photos, accurate quotations, and careful coverage of student athletes.
